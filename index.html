About Bzzy

Two friends.
One simple idea.

Bzzy is being built by two friends who wanted booking a landscaper to feel as easy as booking a restaurant reservation. On the web, iOS, and Android, clients can search by service, price, location, and availability, then compare contractor profiles, portfolios, reviews, pricing, and completed work. It is designed to help people find the right contractor quickly, even when the work needs to happen tomorrow or later in the week.

For contractors, Bzzy brings profiles, services, pricing, availability, leads, and scheduling into one place. Jobber and Google Calendar connections make it easier to bring existing clients and schedules into Bzzy.

Free accounts include profile analytics and lead insights. Profile boosts, subscriptions, and advanced search tools help contractors find the opportunities that fit their business.
