Frequently asked

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Partnerships are at an early stage. Here is what that means today.

What is Bzzy?

Bzzy is an emerging marketplace for landscaping services. It is designed to help clients discover and connect with contractors, while giving contractors tools for opportunities, availability, schedules, and client conversations.

Who can become a partner?

We welcome conversations with landscaping supply stores, equipment dealerships, material suppliers, nurseries, irrigation suppliers, and industry software or service providers.

Does Bzzy currently offer advertising?

Not as a self-service or broadly available product. Bzzy is currently discussing potential founding partnerships and limited pilots. Any proposed placement would be clearly defined before participation.

How will promotions reach contractors?

Possible formats include clearly identified supplier placements, contractor offers, and regionally relevant promotions. These are concepts under evaluation, not guaranteed inventory.

Can local suppliers participate?

Yes. Local and regional relevance is an important part of the conversation, especially for suppliers serving defined geographic markets.

Is there a cost to become a founding partner?

There is no cost to request a conversation. Any future paid pilot, placement, or program would be discussed transparently and agreed separately before it begins.

How do I request a demo?

Complete the partnership inquiry form and mention your interest in a product walkthrough. The Bzzy team will follow up using your business email.
